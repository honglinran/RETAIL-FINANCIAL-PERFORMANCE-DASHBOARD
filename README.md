# Retail Industry Power BI Dashboard: Financial & Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is a **retail industry financial analysis dashboard** built in **Power BI**. The dashboard was designed to help business users understand performance from different business perspectives, including **finance, sales, marketing, and supply chain**.

The main aim of this project was not only to create visual reports, but also to practise how a data analyst thinks through business questions, prepares data, builds a reliable data model, and presents insights clearly for stakeholders.

> **Note:** This repository is for portfolio demonstration purposes only. The original dataset and business details are treated as confidential, so only dashboard screenshots and project documentation are included.

## Business Questions Answered

This dashboard was designed to support questions such as:

- Which customers, products, and regions generate the highest net sales?
- How is gross margin performing across products and markets?
- Which products or customers are underperforming compared with last year?
- How accurate is the forecast across customers and product segments?
- Where are the biggest supply chain risks, such as out-of-order or excess inventory?
- How do sales and margin performance vary across regions, segments, and product categories?
- Which business areas need stakeholder attention or further investigation?

---

## Key Features

- **Interactive slicers** for region, market, customer, segment, category, product, fiscal year, quarter, YTD and YTG views.
- **Financial KPIs** including net sales, gross margin percentage, profit and loss values, year-on-year change, and target comparison.
- **Supply chain KPIs** including forecast accuracy, net error, absolute error, and risk classification.
- **Performance matrix visuals** to compare customers and products by sales and margin performance.
- **Top and bottom analysis** for products, customers, regions, and segments.
- **Dynamic filtering** across multiple business views.
- **Clean dashboard layout** with a business portfolio style and user-focused navigation.
